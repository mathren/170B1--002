* Key concepts

** Week 1
*Reading:* Chapters 1, 2

*** Questions/concepts:
- Relative sizes of the Universe/Galaxy/Solar System/Earth
- What is a light-year?
- How can we see into the past?
- Ages and relative ages of the Universe/Earth/humanity
- Celestial motions:
  - What is the ecliptic?
  - Why do the stars go around the sky?
  - Why can you never see some stars from Tucson?
- Seasons:
  - What causes the seasons on Earth?
  - What effects change the amount of sunlight received over the year?
  - How does the Earth-Sun distance change over the year?
- Lunar phases:
  - What determines the phase of the moon?
  - Relate positions of Moon/Earth/Sun in each phase
- Eclipses:
  - What is a Lunar/Solar eclipse?
  - When do they happen? How often?
  - What moon phases are involved?

** Week 2
*Reading:* Chapter 3, lab 1
*** Questions/concepts:
- Planetary Motion
  - What is retrograde motion?
  - Which planets can move this way?
  - Why was it hard to explain this in early models of the “universe”?
- What is the geocentric model of the universe?
  - How did it explain retrograde motion?
- What were ancient Greek contributions to the development of science?
- What are the hallmarks of science?
- Copernicus/Tycho/Kepler/Galileo
  - Who were they? Roughly when did they live?
  - What were their main contributions?
  - What evidence did Galileo provide for the heliocentric model?
- Kepler’s Laws of planetary motion:
  - What are they?
  - Make sure you can apply them to our Solar System
- How is the process of science illustrated in the story of our model of the Solar System?
- Motion/Momentum/Angular Momentum/Force/Weight
  - What do each of these concepts mean?
  - How do force/acceleration/velocity relate?

** Week 3
*Reading:* Chapter 4, 5
*** Questions/concepts:
- Newton
  - Who was he? Roughly when did he live?
  - What were his major contributions?
  - How is the moon’s orbit like an apple falling?
- Newton’s Laws of Motion
  - What are Newton’s three Laws of Motion?
  - How do they explain forces between objects and the relative
    accelerations of objects of different masses?
  - How does gravitational force depend on mass and distance?
- What is the origin of the tides?
  - What force and what bodies are responsible?
- Conservation Laws:
  - What is a conservation law?
  - What is angular momentum? How does it relate to orbits?
  - What are astronomically relevant forms of energy?
  - What are examples of conversion between those forms of energy?
** Weeks 4 & 5
*Reading:* Chapters 5, 6, lab2
*** Questions/concepts:
- Wave/Particle Light
  - How is light a wave?
  - How are frequency and wavelength related?
  - How is light a particle (photon)?
  - How are frequency and photon energy related?
- Electromagnetic spectrum
  - Which are the most/least energetic?
  - How do light and matter interact?
  - Why is the Sun yellow?
  - Why is a tree green?
- Atoms and Energy Levels
  - Why do atoms emit/absorb in spectral lines?
  - What changes in an atom when a photon is absorbed/emitted?
  - What types of spectra are there? How is each made?
- Continuous spectra
  - What is a blackbody?
  - How does the blackbody spectral peak depend on temperature?
  - How does blackbody luminosity depend on temperature?
- Learning from spectra
  - How do spectra tell us what elements are present?
  - How do we measure velocity from spectral lines?
  - How do we measure temperature from spectra?
- Telescopes
  - Why do we need telescopes?
  - What determines where we put them?
  - What are the important properties and advantages of telescopes?
