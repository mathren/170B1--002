* Galaxies other than the MW

#+CAPTION: /JWST/ deep field showing a field of galaxies
[[./images/JWST_deep_field.jpg]]

A decade ago, the Hubble space telescope (named after the person who
measured the distance to M31 using Leavitt's law for Cepheids) was
pointed for a very long time to /nothing/. This crazy observational
program revealed faint and far galaxies, creating the "/HST/ deep field"
images. Since then, this experiment has been repeated, increasing
exposures and thus depth of the observations, and re-done with /JWST/,
which was in part designed with the scientific goal of studying
galaxies in the early Universe.
