* Pseudoscience vs. Science

|------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------|
| Science                                                                | Pseudoscience                                               |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------|
| based on objective observations                                        | not necessarily (e.g., interpretation of coffee left-overs) |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------|
| makes quantitative and testable predictions                            | makes vague predictions adaptable to many outcomes          |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------|
| Prefers the simple explanations exclusively based on natural phenomena | does not change in the face of new evidence                 |                                                                        |                                                             |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------|
