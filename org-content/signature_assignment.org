** Observations with 21-inch telescope (eyepiece)

The Raymond E. White, Jr. (21-Inch) Telescope in the historic Steward
Observatory Dome will be available for eyepiece observing.

Starting <2026-09-21 Mon> the telescope will be *open every Monday
through Thursday* (excluding Veteran’s Day & Thanksgiving) *from 7:30 –
10:30 p.m.* The last night of operation will be <2026-12-08 Tue>.

You can sign up at [[https://www.signupgenius.com/go/10C0F4EACA922A5F4C70-65656747-21inch][this link]], which requires you to provide an email
so the telescope operators can let you know if the telescope will be
closed because of weather.
