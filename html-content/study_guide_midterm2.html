Study guide for 2nd midterm on 2026-11-04 Wed

The midterm will be administered in our usual classroom (N210) via TopHat and is closed book. You will be allowed one 3” x 5” handwritten notecard (double sided).

Neev will hold a review session in the Parker Library (Steward N304) on 2026-11-02 Mon from 5-6:30pm.

This outline is organized according to the lectures (see also schedule). However, you are responsible for more material than is represented by the lectures. In particular, study:

  • Lecture notes
  • Weekly homework assignments and your Mastering Astronomy. The "Study Area" is very useful for practice!
  • Textbook: The Cosmic Perspective, Chapters 7-18 (eText access via D2L).
