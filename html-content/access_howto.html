Syllabus How to access Schedule

Course materials are being delivered digitally via D2L through the “Pay One Price” program. If you are registered for a main campus undergraduate course, you are automatically enrolled in Pay One Price each semester. Please access the material through Brightspace on the first day of class to make sure that there are no issues with delivery so any problems can be addressed quickly. You can opt out via Brightspace to receive a refund by September 6th, 2026. Note that if you choose to opt out, you will have to purchase both the textbook and Mastering Astronomy to continue in this course – if you are taking a full course load, we encourage you to remain opted-in to Pay One Price, as it is unlikely that you will be able to purchase all materials for all of your courses for less than $275.

How to access course material from D2L

The following steps describe how to access the course material from your browser and within D2L. This is known to work best on Firefox or Chrome/ium and derivatives (avoid using Safari).

  1. Log in to D2L course website.
  2. Go to "Content" (top bar).
  3. On the left, click "Pay One Price and Inclusive Access Materials".

  4. Under it, click "Textbook Access or Opt Out".

  5. On the right now, you should see and click on

  6. This will open a new tab in your browser, directing you to the course materials, click "Open" on the right.
  7. Finally, click on "Open Mylab & Mastering".
