How to access course material from D2L

The following steps describe how to access the course material from your browser and within D2L. This is known to work best on Firefox or Chrome/ium and derivatives (avoid using Safari).

  1. Log in to D2L course website.
  2. Go to "Content" and on the left, click "Pay One Price and Inclusive Access Materials"
  3. Under it, click "Textbook Access or Opt Out".
  4. On the right now, you should see and click on
  5. This will open a new tab in your browser, directing you to the course materials, click "Open" on the right.
  6. Finally, click on "Open Mylab & Mastering".
