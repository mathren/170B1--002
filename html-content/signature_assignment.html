Logistics

Observations with 21-inch telescope (eyepiece)

The Raymond E. White, Jr. (21-Inch) Telescope in the historic Steward Observatory Dome will be available for eyepiece observing.

Starting 2026-09-21 Mon the telescope will be open every Monday through Thursday (excluding Veteran’s Day & Thanksgiving) from 7:30 – 10:30 p.m. The last night of operation will be 2026-12-08 Tue.

You can sign up at this link, which requires you to provide an email so the telescope operators can let you know if the telescope will be closed because of weather.

Observation with Seestar S50 camera

To observe with the Seestar S50 camera, sign up at this link. Slots to observe start on 2026-09-28 Mon and end on 2026-12-03 Thu.
