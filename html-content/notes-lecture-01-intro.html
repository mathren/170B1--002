Introduction to Astronomy

The idea is to introduce the scientific method through an overview of astronomy/astrophysics.

There will be some math (not much more than multiplications and divisions, but with sometimes very large and very small numbers!), since this is necessary for science, which is a quantitative effort to understand the world.
